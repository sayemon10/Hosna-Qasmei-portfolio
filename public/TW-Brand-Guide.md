# Timeo Williams Brand Guide
## Citizen-Warrior · Technologist

### Brand Identity
**Archetype:** Citizen-Warrior · Technologist  
**Tagline:** "Building Systems for Stronger Citizens"  
**Mission:** Disciplined strength, civic responsibility, and mastery of systems

### Design Principles

#### 1. Authenticity → "Rooted in Duty and Discipline"
- **Visual Language:** Military precision meets Swiss typography
- **Color Palette:** Army hues (olive, slate, bone white) balanced by modern graphite/titanium accents
- **Tone:** Calm, deliberate, mission-oriented; no hyperbole, no hustle culture

#### 2. Timeless Design → "Precision Meets Endurance"
- **Typography:** Helvetica Neue / Akkurat / Neue Haas Unica
- **Grid:** Swiss modular layout with military alignment
- **Motion:** Subtle transitions; command-line-like precision
- **Test:** Design should look equally powerful in 2035

#### 3. Behavioral Coherence → "Live Like You Code"
- **Social Tone:** Write like a strategist-engineer: crisp syntax, no filler
- **Posting Rhythm:** Consistent cadence — weekly logs or reflections
- **Work Habits:** Show process, not just outcomes

### Color Palette

#### Primary Colors
- **Bone White:** #F5F5F3 (main logo color)
- **Graphite:** #2C2C2C (dark backgrounds)
- **Titanium:** #E8E8E8 (light accents)

#### Secondary Colors
- **Olive:** #8B8B5A (military reference)
- **Slate:** #4A4A4A (neutral dark)
- **Steel:** #B8B8B8 (subtle borders)

### Typography

#### Primary Font Stack
```css
font-family: 'Helvetica Neue', 'Akkurat', 'Neue Haas Unica', system-ui, sans-serif;
```

#### Usage Guidelines
- **Headings:** Bold weight, generous spacing
- **Body:** Regular weight, crisp readability
- **Code:** Monospace for technical content
- **Quotes:** Serif for emphasis and authority

### Usage Guidelines

#### Do's
- Use the monogram with generous whitespace
- Maintain consistent color application
- Respect the structural grid system
- Apply military precision to all layouts

#### Don'ts
- Don't distort or rotate the monogram
- Don't use colors outside the approved palette
- Don't add decorative elements to the logo
- Don't use outdated versions

### Brand Evolution
This monogram represents the evolution from "TM" to "TW" - a shift from generic initials to a purposeful brand mark that embodies the Citizen-Warrior Technologist archetype. The design balances military discipline with technological precision, creating a timeless symbol of strength, duty, and innovation.
